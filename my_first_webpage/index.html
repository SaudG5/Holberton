Welcome to My First Webpage

In this page I will introduce you to a hobby I love, 3D Printing!

What is 3D Printing?

3D printing is a technology that creates physical objects from digital designs by building them layer by layer using materials like plastic or metal.

Where is 3D printing used today?

Some of the examples:
- In medicine: to make prosthetics
- In engineering: to build prototypes
- In architecture: to build entire buildings!

How do you start this hobby?

You can design anything and hold it in your hands just hours later!
